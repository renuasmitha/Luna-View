About & data

Calculated values (this app)

Low-precision analytic model, run in your browser. Sun: sub-solar longitude from the mean synodic month (29.5306 d, epoch 2000-01-06 18:14 UTC), sub-solar latitude from the Sun–lunar-node angle (±1.54°). Earth: optical libration in latitude (±6.7°, argument of latitude) and longitude (±6.3°, mean anomaly). Elevation = asin of the direction vector dotted with the local “up” at the site. Uncertainties of a degree or more are plausible, which matters near the pole where elevations are a few degrees. Lunar-orbit eccentricity, Earth’s and the Sun’s angular size, refraction-free vacuum and a spherical Moon are assumed. Terrain is not modelled; the horizon mask is a user-set flat obstruction angle.

Site coordinates

Approximate values from public references for demonstration. Verify against LOLA / Moon Trek before relying on them. “Custom” is whatever you type.

Backend & data status

With the FastAPI backend running: Sun and Earth elevations come from the JPL Horizons API (observer on the lunar surface, airless apparent elevation); if Horizons is unreachable the backend falls back to the analytic model and the source chip says so. The rescheduling sweep always uses the local model. Optional: LLM assistant via a server-side key. Not integrated: LOLA terrain and horizon masks, SPICE kernels, a 3D terrain globe. Use these for validated values: JPL Horizons · NAIF SPICE · LOLA (PDS) · Moon Trek.
